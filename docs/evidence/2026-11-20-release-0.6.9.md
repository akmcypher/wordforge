two-column regression failed before the version predicate was fixed.

## Scope limits and evidence

This remains a developer preview: partial DOCX, unsupported body content warnings,
plain-text notes/comments, no decimal/bar tabs, tab ruler or complete RTL tab geometry,
no table-boundary keep layout, widow/orphan control, incremental/background pagination
or full accessibility certification. These are not inferred from suite counts.
