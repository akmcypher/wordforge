Opens tables and pictures from Word documents, adds headers, footers and page numbers,
and lets you merge table cells and set column widths.

## Downloads

**Windows 10 (1903+) and Windows 11**

* `Wordforge-0.6.4-windows-x64-setup.exe` — installer for most PCs
* `Wordforge-0.6.4-windows-arm64-setup.exe` — installer for Arm PCs (Snapdragon and similar)

The installer needs no administrator rights. It adds a Start menu entry and an
uninstaller (Settings → Apps), opens `.wfg` files in Wordforge and adds Wordforge to
"Open with" for `.docx` and `.txt`.

Portable versions, nothing to install: `Wordforge-0.6.4-windows-x64.zip` and
`Wordforge-0.6.4-windows-arm64.zip`. Unzip the whole folder, then run `Wordforge.exe`.

The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first
launch (More info → Run anyway). Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Ubuntu (amd64, arm64)**: `sudo snap install wordforge` (https://snapcraft.io/wordforge)

## Changes since 0.6.3

* **Tables and pictures from Word documents.** Opening a DOCX now keeps its tables (up
  to 10 columns), including merged cells and column widths, and its pictures (PNG, JPEG,
  GIF, BMP and other common formats), scaled to fit the page. Nested tables are
  flattened into their cell, and floating pictures are placed in the text, each with a
  warning.
* **Headers, footers and page numbers.** Insert → Header and footer… sets left, centre
  and right text for the top and bottom of every page. *Insert page number* and *Insert
  page count* add `{PAGE}` and `{NUMPAGES}` (for example "Page {PAGE} of {NUMPAGES}"),
  and you can leave the first page without them. They print and export to PDF, and are
  kept in DOCX files both ways.
* **Merged cells and column widths.** Format → Table → Merge cells (select from one cell
  into another), Split merged cell, Column width… and Distribute columns evenly. Merging
  moves the text into the top-left cell, as in Word. Merges and widths are kept in DOCX
  files both ways.

Documents with headers, footers, merged cells or custom column widths are saved as WFG8,
which 0.6.3 cannot open. Other documents keep their earlier format.

## Known limitations

* Headers and footers are plain text in one line; no per-section or even/odd-page
  variants, formatting or pictures, and no fields other than page number and count.
* Tables: no dragging of column borders, row heights, cell shading or borders; more than
  10 columns are imported as paragraphs.
* Pictures cannot float or wrap text.
* No footnotes, comments or tracked changes yet.
* Word features Wordforge does not understand are not preserved on DOCX export.
* The new DOCX features have not yet been checked in Microsoft Word or LibreOffice.
* Visual caret movement through mixed-direction text and screen-reader support are
  incomplete.
* Windows packages are not code-signed and have not been tested on a fresh Windows 10
  installation.
* The Snap's strict confinement is tested headless in CI, not yet interactively on a
  real Ubuntu desktop.

Please report problems at https://github.com/akmcypher/wordforge/issues.
