Adds your own paragraph and character styles, and keeps them when you save to or open
Word documents.

## Downloads

**Windows 10 (1903+) and Windows 11**

* `Wordforge-0.6.3-windows-x64-setup.exe` — installer for most PCs
* `Wordforge-0.6.3-windows-arm64-setup.exe` — installer for Arm PCs (Snapdragon and similar)

The installer needs no administrator rights. It adds a Start menu entry and an
uninstaller (Settings → Apps), opens `.wfg` files in Wordforge and adds Wordforge to
"Open with" for `.docx` and `.txt`.

Portable versions, nothing to install: `Wordforge-0.6.3-windows-x64.zip` and
`Wordforge-0.6.3-windows-arm64.zip`. Unzip the whole folder, then run `Wordforge.exe`.

The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first
launch (More info → Run anyway). Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Ubuntu (amd64, arm64)**: `sudo snap install wordforge` (https://snapcraft.io/wordforge)

## Changes since 0.6.2

* **Your own paragraph styles.** Format → Styles → New style from selection turns a
  paragraph's formatting into a named style; Rename style and Delete style manage it.
  The style box lists the built-in styles, then yours.
* **Character styles.** Select a word or phrase and use Format → Styles → New character
  style from selection. Character styles appear after a separator in the style box, sit
  on top of the paragraph style (so they keep a heading's size), and can be removed with
  Remove character style or Clear formatting.
* **Styles in Word documents.** DOCX export writes your paragraph and character styles
  with their names, so Word shows them; Heading 1 and 2 are recognised by Word's
  navigation pane. Opening a DOCX keeps the custom styles the document uses.

Documents with character styles are saved as WFG7, which 0.6.2 cannot open; documents
with only paragraph styles stay readable by 0.6.2.

## Known limitations

* No ready-made character styles (such as Strong or Emphasis) yet; create your own.
* Exported styles have not yet been checked in Microsoft Word or LibreOffice.
* No headers and footers, footnotes, comments or tracked changes yet.
* Tables cannot merge cells or set column widths; DOCX tables and pictures are not
  imported; floating or wrapped pictures are not supported.
* Word features Wordforge does not understand are not preserved on DOCX export.
* Visual caret movement through mixed-direction text and screen-reader support are
  incomplete.
* Windows packages are not code-signed and have not been tested on a fresh Windows 10
  installation.
* The Snap's strict confinement is tested headless in CI, not yet interactively on a
  real Ubuntu desktop.

Please report problems at https://github.com/akmcypher/wordforge/issues.
