# Wordforge 0.6.9

Sections and columns, aligned contents entries and paragraph pagination controls,
with toolbar and initial typing-format fixes.

## Changes since 0.6.8

- **Sections and columns:** insert next-page or continuous section breaks; choose
  portrait/landscape, page size, margins and equal columns for each section.
- **Aligned contents entries:** generated/updated tables of contents use right-aligned
  page numbers and dot leaders. Left, centre and right paragraph tabs round-trip
  through Wordforge and DOCX. There is no tab-ruler editing UI yet.
- **Paragraph pagination:** Format panel options keep headings with following text
  and keep short paragraphs together. Explicit breaks take priority; groups too
  large for a page split. Columns and footnote reservations participate.
- **Toolbar and typing:** labels fit their controls and use overflow in narrow
  windows. Opening a formatted document initializes the font controls and immediate
  typing from the initial caret's formatting.
- **DOCX fidelity:** keep-next/keep-lines retain style inheritance and explicit
  true/false overrides. Unsupported custom automatic tab intervals are preserved in
  settings but display at 36 pt, with a warning.

## Downloads

Windows x64 and ARM64 installers and portable ZIPs are published with checksums.
The installers are per-user and unsigned; trusted code signing is not configured.
Windows security warnings may still appear. No warning-free distribution is claimed.

Snap packages for amd64 and ARM64 use version `0.6.9-preview`. Stable channel
promotion follows successful package builds and strict-confinement checks; verify
the channel state before assuming that the newest release is available there.

Native documents use WFG13 for sections, WFG14 for tab stops, or WFG15 for pagination
settings (including tab stops when both are used). The reader supports WFG1–15.
Older applications can refuse newer documents; preserve copies when exchanging files.

## Known limitations

This remains a developer preview with partial DOCX support. Unknown body content
can be omitted with a warning; this is not full DOCX preservation. Notes/comments
are plain text. Decimal/bar tabs, other tab leaders and RTL-specific tab geometry
are unsupported. Keep-next across table boundaries is preserved with a warning but
not laid out. Columns are not balanced; section headers remain document-wide.
Widow/orphan control, incremental/background pagination and complete screen-reader
support remain future work. Word 97–2003 .doc, RTF and .odt are unsupported.

Automated structural Word checks do not certify visual parity. See the compatibility
matrix and evidence files for platform-specific results and remaining checks.
