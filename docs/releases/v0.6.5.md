Adds footnotes and comments, and keeps more of a Word document when you open and save it.

## Downloads

**Windows 10 (1903+) and Windows 11**

* `Wordforge-0.6.5-windows-x64-setup.exe` — installer for most PCs
* `Wordforge-0.6.5-windows-arm64-setup.exe` — installer for Arm PCs (Snapdragon and similar)

The installer needs no administrator rights. It adds a Start menu entry and an
uninstaller (Settings → Apps), opens `.wfg` files in Wordforge and adds Wordforge to
"Open with" for `.docx` and `.txt`.

Portable versions, nothing to install: `Wordforge-0.6.5-windows-x64.zip` and
`Wordforge-0.6.5-windows-arm64.zip`. Unzip the whole folder, then run `Wordforge.exe`.

The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first
launch (More info → Run anyway). Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Ubuntu (amd64, arm64)**: `sudo snap install wordforge` (https://snapcraft.io/wordforge)

## Changes since 0.6.4

* **Footnotes.** Insert → Footnote… (Ctrl+Alt+F) adds a numbered superscript reference
  at the cursor; the note appears at the bottom of the same page, under a short rule,
  and pages make room for it. Insert → Edit footnote… changes it; deleting the number
  deletes the note. Footnotes print, export to PDF, and are kept in DOCX files both
  ways.
* **Comments.** Select text and use Insert → Comment… (Ctrl+Alt+M). Commented text is
  highlighted, and the Comments panel (View → Comments panel) lists every comment with
  its author; click one to jump to its text, or edit and delete it there. Comments move
  with the text as you edit and are kept in DOCX files both ways.
* **More of your Word document is kept.** Opening and saving a DOCX now keeps its
  document properties (title, author), theme, settings, font table and custom data
  exactly as they were, instead of dropping them. The open prompt now names the parts
  Wordforge still cannot keep.

Documents with footnotes, comments or kept Word parts are saved as WFG9, which 0.6.4
cannot open. Other documents keep their earlier format.

## Known limitations

* Footnotes and comments are plain text, one paragraph each; no endnotes, comment
  replies or resolved state, and no tracked changes yet.
* Word content inside the text that Wordforge does not understand (endnotes, fields,
  tracked changes) is still left out when saving, with a warning when you open the file.
* Headers and footers are plain text in one line; tables have no row heights, shading or
  draggable borders; pictures cannot float or wrap text.
* The new DOCX features have not yet been checked in Microsoft Word or LibreOffice.
* Visual caret movement through mixed-direction text and screen-reader support are
  incomplete.
* Windows packages are not code-signed and have not been tested on a fresh Windows 10
  installation.
* The Snap's strict confinement is tested headless in CI, not yet interactively on a
  real Ubuntu desktop.

Please report problems at https://github.com/akmcypher/wordforge/issues.
