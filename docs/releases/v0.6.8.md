Links, endnotes, a table of contents and Word's fields, plus Word's own heading styles and
theme colours. On 114 test documents built by Microsoft Word, Wordforge now scores 100/100
(rounded from 99.6).

## Downloads

**Windows 10 (1903+) and Windows 11**

* `Wordforge-0.6.8-windows-x64-setup.exe` — installer for most PCs
* `Wordforge-0.6.8-windows-arm64-setup.exe` — installer for Arm PCs (Snapdragon and similar)

The installer needs no administrator rights. It adds a Start menu entry and an
uninstaller (Settings → Apps), opens `.wfg` files in Wordforge and adds Wordforge to
"Open with" for `.docx` and `.txt`.

Portable versions, nothing to install: `Wordforge-0.6.8-windows-x64.zip` and
`Wordforge-0.6.8-windows-arm64.zip`. Unzip the whole folder, then run `Wordforge.exe`.

The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first
launch (More info → Run anyway). Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Ubuntu (amd64, arm64)**: `sudo snap install wordforge` (https://snapcraft.io/wordforge)

## Changes since 0.6.7

* **Links.** Insert → Link… (Ctrl+Shift+K) links the selected text to a web page, an e-mail
  address or a bookmark, or inserts new linked text. Ctrl+click opens a link, hovering shows
  where it goes, and right-click offers Open, Edit and Remove link. Typing inside a link
  extends it; typing after it does not. Links travel to and from Word and through copy and
  paste. Unsafe link types (such as `javascript:`) are refused.
* **Endnotes.** Insert → Endnote… (Ctrl+Alt+D, as in Word). Endnotes are numbered i, ii, iii
  and listed after the text, continuing onto new pages when long. Word endnotes used to be
  dropped when opening.
* **Table of contents.** Insert → Table of contents builds one from your Title and Heading
  1/2 paragraphs, with page numbers; Insert → Update table of contents rebuilds it after
  you edit.
* **Word fields are kept.** Tables of contents, page references and other Word fields keep
  their codes when you open and save a Word file; their text stays editable.
* **Word headings and colours.** Word documents keep Word's own Normal, Title and Heading
  styles (paragraph ends no longer turn Arial bold) and Word's theme colours, so text keeps
  its colour when the document's theme is used again in Word.

How this was measured: Word built 114 test documents, Wordforge opened and saved each, and
Word compared the results. Since 0.6.7: character formatting 89.5% → 99.5%, header,
footer, note and comment text 95.9% → 100%, document structure 97.9% → 99.7%, score 97 →
100 (rounded). Details: docs/WORD_FIDELITY.md.

Documents with links, endnotes, fields or Word theme colours are saved as WFG12, which
0.6.7 cannot open. Other documents keep their earlier format.

## Known limitations

* Word 97–2003 `.doc`, RTF and OpenDocument `.odt` files do not open yet.
* Word section settings (orientation, columns, per-section margins) are lost, with a
  warning when you open the file.
* Footnotes, endnotes and comments are plain text, one paragraph each. Table of contents
  entries are plain lines; their page numbers are not right-aligned with dot leaders, and
  other fields (such as PAGE in the text) keep the result Word last showed.
* Nested list levels are indented but use one marker style. Borders are one width and
  colour per table.
* LibreOffice has not been tested yet; the checks above used Microsoft Word.
* Encoding detection can guess wrong for very short legacy text files.
* Tracked changes cover typed and deleted text only.
* Visual caret movement through mixed-direction text and screen-reader support are
  incomplete.
* Windows packages are not code-signed and have not been tested on a fresh Windows 10
  installation. The Snap's strict confinement is tested headless in CI, not yet
  interactively on a real Ubuntu desktop.

Please report problems at https://github.com/akmcypher/wordforge/issues.
